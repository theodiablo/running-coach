🏃 Running Coach — Privacy Policy

A free, open-source running-training app. No ads, no upsells, and no selling your data.

Last updated: 23 July 2026

Who we are

Running Coach is an independent, open-source project. The source code is public, so you can verify exactly what is stored and how. This policy covers both the web app (run.camboulive.solutions) and the Android app.

What we collect

  • Your email address — to create and sign in to your account.
  • Your runs — date, distance, duration, heart rate, effort, and notes.
  • Your training plan and race settings.
  • Your name, if you choose to enter one during onboarding.
  • Coach memory, if you choose to save notes for future AI coach chats, such as training preferences, schedule constraints, or injury context.
  • GPS location and route traces, when you use live run tracking (see below).
  • Heart-rate data — measured by a paired Bluetooth heart-rate sensor, or read from Android Health Connect, if you turn either on (Android only; see below).

Location data & background tracking

When you start a live run, the app records your precise GPS location to draw your route and compute distance, pace, and elevation. In the Android app this includes collecting location in the background — while the app is closed or the screen is off — so your run keeps recording.

  • Location is collected only while a run is actively recording. Stop the run and collection stops. The app never tracks you in the background at any other time.
  • A persistent notification is shown the entire time a run is recording, so it is always clear when location is being collected.
  • Your route is saved to your own account and is shown only to you.

Sharing a run live

You can optionally share a run as it happens. This is off by default. You turn it on on the device you are recording with, before you press Start. That device remembers the choice for your next runs — it is shown to you on the start screen every time, and stays visible on screen the whole time a run is being shared, so you can always see and change it. The choice is never carried over to your other devices.

  • While it is on, your position and the route so far are sent to our server during the run, so that your own other signed-in devices can follow along. By default no one else can see it.
  • You can additionally create a share link for a run, one run at a time, and send it to people you choose. Anyone holding that link can watch that run as it happens. The link stops working when the run ends (and you can cut it off earlier at any time, without stopping the recording). Each run gets a fresh link — an old link never shows a new run.
  • The shared copy is deleted as soon as you save or discard the run. If the app is closed by your phone mid-run, it is deleted the next time you open the app on that device, and your other devices stop showing it after six hours. What remains afterwards is the ordinary saved run and route, visible only to you.
  • With sharing off, your position is never sent anywhere during a run. The route is uploaded only when you save it.
  • This is not a safety or emergency feature. Updates depend on your phone having signal and can stop without warning, so never rely on it to get help.

Heart-rate sensors & Health Connect (Android)

On the Android app you can optionally capture your heart rate during a run, so you don't have to type it in. This is off by default; you turn it on in Settings → Profile → Heart-rate sensor.

  • Bluetooth sensor. If you pair a Bluetooth heart-rate sensor (a chest strap, an armband, or a watch that broadcasts heart rate), the app reads your heart rate from it only while a run is recording. Bluetooth is used solely to find and read your sensor — never to determine your location.
  • Health Connect. If you choose Health Connect, the app reads your heart rate from Android's Health Connect only for the time period of a run you recorded, after you finish it. It requests read access to heart rate — and nothing else.
  • Heart-rate readings are saved on your run, in your own account, exactly like a value you type in. They are not shared with any third party, not used for advertising, and not sold. We do not transfer Health Connect data anywhere beyond storing it in your account.
  • You can turn either source off at any time in Settings, and revoke access in your Android Bluetooth or Health Connect settings. Deleting a run — or your account — deletes its heart-rate data too.

How your data is stored

Your account and data are stored using Supabase (authentication and a per-user database). Every user's data is isolated by row-level security, so no other user can read it. Map tiles are served by MapTiler; rendering a map sends the relevant map-tile coordinates to MapTiler, but not your account identity.

AI coach

The AI coach is powered by Anthropic Claude. When you use the coach, your message, current plan, goal, recent run summaries, and saved Coach memory may be sent to Anthropic to generate a response. Coach conversations, proposals, tool calls, and the context the model saw are stored in your account's Supabase-backed audit logs so the app can validate proposals and improve the coach. If you use "This isn't right", your correction may also be emailed to the maintainer for review.

The AI coach may suggest a Coach memory note, but it is saved only if you choose Save to memory. You can edit or delete Coach memory at any time in Settings → Coach memory. The coach is not medical advice.

How your data is used

Your data is used only to run the app for you — to build your plan, log and display your runs, show your routes, and answer AI coach requests you send.

  • We do not sell your data.
  • We do not share it with third parties for advertising.
  • There is no advertising or behavioural tracking.
  • Optional, opt-in pseudonymous analytics and crash diagnostics may be collected through PostHog if you choose to share them in the app. These events do not include routes, run notes, heart-rate samples, or coach message text.

Your rights & deleting your data

You can view and delete individual runs (and their routes) at any time inside the app. To delete your entire account and all associated data, open the web app at run.camboulive.solutions and go to Settings → Delete account. This permanently and immediately removes your account, all runs, GPS routes, training plan, Coach memory, coach logs, and settings — it cannot be undone. You can also export an app backup from Settings before deleting. If you would rather we do it for you, email the address below and we will remove it manually.

Permissions (Android)

  • Location (precise, incl. background) — to record your run route, including with the screen off. Requested only when you start tracking.
  • Notifications — to show the persistent "recording run" notification.
  • Nearby devices (Bluetooth) — to find and connect to a heart-rate sensor. Used only while a run is recording, and never for location. Requested only if you set up a Bluetooth sensor.
  • Health Connect (read heart rate) — to read your heart rate for a recorded run. Requested only if you choose Health Connect as your heart-rate source.

Children

Running Coach is not directed at children under 13, and we do not knowingly collect data from them.
